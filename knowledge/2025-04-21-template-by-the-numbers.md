# You'll Never Build Commander Decks the Same Way Again

Source: Based Deck Department, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwTCvRTunKQ (transcript only). Maths credited to Frank Karsten.

- **Start from the game plan.** Know how the deck plays its perfect game and by which turn it needs each kind of card. Then set counts from that, per deck.
- **Lands.** Three lands in the opening hand and five by turn 5 needs 42 to 43 lands with no ramp. 38 hits land drops through turn 4 and suits over 80% of decks. 36 for extra gas is acceptable; 32 is not.
- **Trading lands for ramp/draw.** Remove one land for every two or three cheap ramp or draw sources added.
- **Ramp is all or nothing.** Wanting ramp by turn 2 needs 11 sources. Four ramp pieces is the same as zero. Some decks correctly run none (2-mana commander, average mana value under 2).
- **Copies needed to have one of an effect:**
  - by turn 2: 11
  - by turn 5: 9
  - by turn 8: 6 (assumes about three extra cards drawn)
  - two copies by turn 8: 11
  - Want more copies at that point, run more.
- **Make counts an active choice.** Running few removal spells is fine if chosen on purpose.
- **Categories must be deck-specific.** Ramp, removal and draw should synergize with the commander, not be the best generic eleven.
- **Double duty creates room.** Cards that fill two categories free slots for the theme.
- **The template forces streamlining.** Pet cards and alternate win conditions take slots the deck needs.
- **Free mulligans and luck are not a plan.** Do not use them to justify low land counts.
- Does not apply to cEDH, where free spells and fast mana change the numbers.
