# 30 Years Of Magic Deck Building Knowledge in 15 minutes

Source: Based Deck Department, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1j1K8nwVlb8 (transcript only)

1. **One big snowball.** Every card supports one win strategy. Several cards may enable it, but the type of win stays the same. Build resilience for the main plan instead of adding alternate win conditions. Applies to combo decks too.
2. **Guaranteed good beats maybe great.** Pick the boring reliable card over the high-upside situational one.
3. **Be unexpected.** Opponents play around staples. Where quality is equal, run the lesser-known version (Tangle over Fog, Massacre Wurm over Toxic Deluge, Daze over Counterspell). Never run a bad card just for surprise.
4. **Cut win-more.** A win-more card needs the deck to already be doing its thing. Fill the deck with cards that get you to the winning state and work when behind; keep only a handful of finishers.
5. **The real game changers are instant-speed mass effects:** combat tricks (Tangle, Aetherize, Arachnogenesis), phasing (Teferi's Protection, Clever Concealment, Galadriel's Dismissal), board protection (Dawn's Truce, Flare of Fortitude, Flawless Maneuver, Heroic Intervention), mass bounce (Evacuation, Engulf the Shore).
6. **Eat your vegetables.** Baseline for almost every deck:
   - 38+ lands (MDFCs count; treat them as spells only when a land would be a dead draw)
   - 10+ ramp
   - 10+ single-target interaction (sorceries and creatures count)
   - 10+ card advantage (draw, recursion, and board wipes all count)
   - 2+ board wipes, asymmetrical where possible
   - Maybe one deck in a collection should break these.
7. **Keep the curve low.** Doing more things per game beats doing bigger things. Sort by mana value and check each slot can be played on curve. Compare proactive against reactive cards per slot: a full 2-drop slot of reactive spells means nothing to do on turn 2. Do not go so low the deck does too little.
8. **No autopilot.** Every card must justify itself in this deck. Examples: Cultivate with a 3-mana commander, ramp when the commander is the ramp, symmetrical wipes in Voltron.
9. **Resilience.** Expect disruption and plan countermeasures: protection, own counters, recursion. About 25 cards are seen in a 10-turn game, so run at least 4 of an effect to have one when needed, and 8 for faster games or repeated disruption.
10. **Card advantage quietly wins games.** Choose the form that fits the deck (go-wide draw, Voltron draw, control draw and wipes).
11. **Bonus: build budget decks.** Fewer options force better choices.
