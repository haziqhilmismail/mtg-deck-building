# You'll Never Build Boring Commander Decks Again

Source: Based Deck Department, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zAze6D_ZBNM (transcript only)

These are fun-first principles. Apply fully for casual brackets; for high-power brackets keep the ones that also raise power (flexible, multi-job, interaction, focus) and treat the rest as tie-breakers.

- **Commander is a shared adventure.** Aim for memorable games, not only wins.
- **Seek novelty.** Pick the most suitable and interesting card for the theme over the strongest generic card in the colours.
- **Play flexible cards.** Modal spells, MDFCs, and cards with several uses (a bounce spell can swing tempo, save your own permanent, stop a combat trigger, or buy an ally).
- **Chase synergy and multi-job cards.** Removal that draws, a creature that blocks and fetches a land, a sacrifice outlet that draws.
- **Play more interaction, especially at instant speed.** Abilities on permanents count. More meaningful actions means more engagement.
- **Build focused decks.** One thing done very well. Want variety, build another deck.
- **Limit the same staples across decks.** Put a card where it synergizes and leave it out elsewhere.
- **Deck-building order:** brainstorm what the commander wants, search Scryfall widely by keyword, and only then check EDHREC or Reddit for misses.
- **Know when to break the rules.** Keep the fundamentals and a smooth curve, but a creature deck can carry its removal on creatures.
- **Play cards you would enjoy losing to.** Avoid miserable play patterns. The author often cuts Sol Ring in casual pods.
